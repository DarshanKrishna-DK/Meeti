# Why Arkiv

Meeti: Good connections begin with a little intent.

Independent clients need to discover the same owner-attributed public intent without trusting a private application database. Arkiv supplies wallet-owned, queryable, expiring entities and public receipt references. The application uses these properties directly: filters execute against typed indexed columns, signed projections bind the publication to its owner, and chain plus application deadlines stop stale matching.

Removing Arkiv disables new cross-user discovery. Existing private account state cannot silently replace its public index. ENS remains the authority for agent capabilities and policy; World remains the protected human approval gate. Arkiv is the discovery boundary and does not replace either provider.

Expiry bounds active availability rather than guaranteeing secure erasure. Public signatures do not verify professional claims or permission to contact someone. Private conversations and contact disclosure remain in the access-controlled server store.
