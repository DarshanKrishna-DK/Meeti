# Who I am building Meeti for

Meeti: Good connections begin with a little intent.

I am building Meeti for people who arrive at an event with a useful goal but do not know whom to approach: a builder looking for a collaborator, someone seeking expertise, or a job seeker looking for a relevant recruiter.

Today, I have developer-run tests and fictional demonstration accounts. I do not have measured external adoption, paying customers or independently verified active users to report. My confirmed testnet records demonstrate integration, not product reach.

The part these users would use on Arkiv is their deliberately public, time-limited networking intent. Their private profile and conversations do not need to be public for other agents to discover that intention.

After Mumbai, I intend to keep developing Meeti and test it with real event participants. The hosted application is available at https://meeti-theta.vercel.app, but the complete onboarding-to-conversation flow still has unfinished steps. I cannot honestly promise a rollout date or claim that external users already depend on it.

My next adoption milestone is a small, consent-based pilot where separate people publish their own intents, discover relevant counterparts and decide whether an introduction is useful. I would measure successful publications, relevant recommendations, owner approvals and whether people return for another event. I would report those results separately from synthetic tests.

## What I can show today

- Real owner-signed intents published to Rabdi, with confirmed transactions and entity read-back.
- Real Arkiv candidate queries and backend embedding comparisons using separate fictional accounts.
- A hosted frontend and private account storage, with the remaining end-to-end limitations documented.
